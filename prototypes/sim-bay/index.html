Rough, playable sketches. Each one is something the story already has the crew do, and each would replace a part of the game today rather than add to it. Sound is off. Numbers and lines are placeholders. Round two: four new ones, two reworked.

    The rule: a minigame earns its place only if the story needs the thing you do in it. Counting, dating, flying, listening, walking.
