How it is drawn

Each body is a skeleton (hips, spine, neck, arms, legs) posed in code every frame. Limbs and torso are shaded tubes, the head is an ellipse with a jaw shape, hair and collars are polygons. Every pixel gets its light from the room lamp, then picks a colour from a short ramp that starts at the near-black, with a little ordered dither. The face is a small hand-placed pixel map on top: eyes, nose, mouth, cheek marks.
