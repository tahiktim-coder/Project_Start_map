For the artists

Your file

  • Edit only your own <name>.js. It calls CrewEngine.register({…}) once.
  • Never change frames, timing or positions. If something looks wrong for everyone, tell the engine owner.
  • Pixel slips on one frame go in fixes: a handful of pixels at most.

Colour

  • Sample the portrait. 5 to 8 colours a ramp, each starting at #05070a.
  • Skin a little warmer than the portrait. Dark cloth lifted (no mid tone under #262f38). Light cloth a step below white.
  • Saturated colour only on the signature detail.

Heads

  • Draw front, down and back by hand, one letter a pixel. lying is optional.
  • 13 to 15 rows crown to chin. Never wider than the shoulders.
  • Check it here at 2× and 1×, lamp and dark, both ways.
