What it costs

How it is drawn

  • Every colour is taken from the portrait PNG: the hair, skin, suit and trim ramps are clusters of its real pixels, darkest first, each starting at the ink #05070a.
  • The head is placed by hand, pixel by pixel, against a shrunken copy of the portrait: Cora 14 × 17 with her collar, Vance 22 × 23 with his ring collar. Hair shape first, then brows, eyes, nose, jaw, and the one signature detail.
  • The body is 15 to 20 simple shapes (tubes for limbs, ovals for chest, waist and hips) hung on a skeleton, lit from one side, snapped to the palette with a light dither, then cleaned: a lit edge toward the light, a dark edge away from it, lone pixels merged.
  • A move is numbers, not drawings: the walk is two foot paths and a bob (the planted foot never slides); the console is two hand targets. The lamp warms whoever stands under it; the screen lights their front.

The cost of the whole cast (5 people × 9 actions)

  • Once, shared: the body renderer and skeleton (done on this page) plus the other 6 actions (climb, sit, sleep, kneel and tend, carry, face the wall). An action is a pose script, so about 1½ to 2 days for all six, including the back view for climbing.
  • Per person: the head by hand, 3 or 4 passes against the portrait (half a day); a back of the head and a bowed head for climbing and the wall (2 to 3 hours); build numbers and costume details (2 hours). About 1 day each, so 3 days for Jaxon, Aris and Mira.
  • Total: about 5 to 6 working days for 5 × 9, plus a polish day once they stand side by side. Painting 48 × 104 frames by hand at this finish would be about 270 frames: several weeks.

Weaknesses

  • The body is smooth and a little soft. Cloth has no real folds, so up close it can read as a figurine. Hand touch-ups per frame would fix it but break the cheap pipeline.
  • The head is a flat sticker in three-quarter view. It does not turn, and facing left is a mirror, so Cora's cheek lines swap cheeks.
  • 14 pixels of head hold the hair, brows, eyes and jaw, not an expression. Moods mean extra heads: about an hour per mood per person.
  • Hair does not move. Aris's loose, flying hair will need a few extra hand-drawn frames.
