Hull B, the Lander

  • It says what the ship is for: land once and become the first house.
  • The wrecks get a new, simple picture: hulls still standing where they came down.
  • The cutaway is already a tower with the nose up. The Lander makes that true.
  • It is one long body, like today's hull, so every place that draws the hull keeps working.

about 16 hours, wrecks and minigames included

Crew option 2, Tall

  • Big enough to tell all five apart from across the panel.
  • Six-frame walk, and room for coats, hair and a tool in hand.
  • Lamplit (3) is the moodiest, but the five blur together in dark rooms.

about 10 hours in ShipCutaway, plus about 4 for the minigames
