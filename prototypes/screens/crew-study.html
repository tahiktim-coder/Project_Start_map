The crew, as sprites

New crew figures built from the five portraits. One skeleton moves everyone the same way; each person has their own costume. Cora is finished as the example. The other four are rough stand-ins until their own pass.

01

Size: 12 × 24

The Tall option (9 × 17) was the right shape but its head is 4 pixels high: not enough for a fringe, a collar and a face. At 12 × 24 the head is 6 rows plus a collar, and the body is 4 heads tall, which still reads as a grown adult.

  • The head gets 6 rows: hair top, fringe, eyes, nose, chin, and the bob ending at the jaw.
  • Room for one signature detail each: Cora's cyan seam, Aris's goggles, Vance's ring collar.
  • Legs are 11 rows, so a 6-frame walk has a real stride and a knee.
  • In a cutaway seen closer (the This War of Mine idea) 24 rows is a person, not a token.

For the next pass

The rules are in docs/CREW_SPRITES.md. The register API is written at the top of this file.

Each character is one file in prototypes/screens/crew/ that calls CrewSprites.register({ … }) with a palette, keys, two head maps, two torso maps and limb materials. Arms, legs, feet, timing and poses come from crew/skeleton.js and are the same for everyone.

Study page only. Nothing in the game is changed. No sound.
