# Silent Exodus: the story on one page (draft A)

## 1. The story
Years after the first golden record, Earth sent a second, better disc into space: where we live, how we build, everything about us.
Then a warning reached Earth from the future, and Earth saw it had shown all its cards to something it did not know.
Earth sent Exodus ship after Exodus ship after the disc and told every crew it was a colony mission; the later ships knew the real order, and they died out here long before us.
The light at the end of our heading learned to want the disc from those crews, and it copies whatever it reads, sure of itself and often wrong.
We on Exodus 9 were never told about the disc, so the light barely notices us, and we are the ones who find it.

## 2. The big moments (in the order the player meets them)
1. **Sector 1, head count.** A.U.R.A. says "four crew" while five sit at their stations. *Something on this ship is already wrong.*
2. **Sector 1, the corridor.** The player flies the heading while the radio counts beacons: eight, then hundreds. *We are not the only ship out here.*
3. **Sector 1, the first wreck.** The player cuts open a dead Exodus with a higher number than ours that is older than us; its orders hold a drawing of a gold disc. *They were sent for something we were never told about.*
4. **Sector 2, the spores.** On a living world, glowing spores drift toward anything warm, and one of the away team carries them home. *Out here, life wants in.*
5. **Sector 2, the garden.** A lawn, a fence, a tree and a bench float in open space and grow toward our hull. *Something has read about Earth and is copying it, badly.*
6. **Sector 3, the sea of wrecks.** The jump stalls, the decks go dark, and every hull ever sent hangs still, with a small light at the end. *They all came this way, and none of them came back.*
7. **Sector 3, the launch broadcast.** A ring of dead ships all play our own launch speech. *Every crew was told the same story we were.*
8. **Sector 4, Eden.** A clean world with a cold stream and fruit; the player may land, settle and end the journey here. *Is this home, or another copy?*
9. **Sector 4, the hole.** Two identical planets sit side by side with a seam between them; fly through and land off every chart beside a wreck sending our own ID. *The copies have edges, and you can fall through them.*
10. **Sector 5, our twin.** A ship exactly like ours flies beside us a moment late, then holds still: four of us wave, one seat is empty. *The light has read us too, and got us wrong by one.*
11. **Sector 6, the structure.** The light fills the windows and gives no heat; A.U.R.A. says "five crew" for the first time, and the light reads everyone except the commander. *We came here by accident, and it hardly knows we exist.*
12. **Sector 6, the disc.** Inside the light lies the real disc, the thing all those ships died for. The one choice: cut it apart so nothing out here can ever read Earth, and lose Earth's only hello forever; or leave it whole, and let the light keep it and keep copying us.

## 3. Under the water (the writers keep this; the player never needs it explained)
- The second disc was a proud hello; the warning that changed Earth's mind came from the future.
- The ships that carried the real order (after 15 or 20, nobody knows; ship 9 was never one) had a newer drive that threw them further into the past, so their wrecks are older than us.
- The light learned to want the disc because every crew that reached it wanted the disc; we do not, so we barely register.
- Why A.U.R.A. counts four while five are aboard is still open.
