# Silent Exodus: the story on one page (2026-10-10)

## 1. The story
Long after the first golden record, Earth sent a second, better disc into space: where we live, how we build, everything about us.
Then a warning reached Earth, and Earth got scared it had shown all its cards to strangers.
Earth sent Exodus ship after Exodus ship to bring the disc back while telling the crews they were going to settle a new world, and the crews who knew the real order died out here.
The light at the end of the road learned from their wanting to want the disc too, and it copies whatever it reads from a ship, sure of itself and wrong.
We on Exodus 9 were never told about the disc, so the light barely notices us, and we find the disc almost by accident.

## 2. The big moments (in the order the player meets them)
1. **Sector 1, the head count.** A.U.R.A. reads out all five of us and says "four crew". *Something on this ship is already wrong.*
2. **Every jump, the corridor.** The player flies the heading through debris while the radio counts distress calls: a few, then hundreds. *We are not the first.*
3. **Sector 1, the first wreck.** The player cuts open a dead Exodus hatch and finds a folded drawing of a gold disc in the logbook. *Why does a colony ship carry this?*
4. **Sector 2, the ring of ships.** Dead ships in a circle all play our own launch broadcast, our families waving. *Every crew was told the same story we were.*
5. **Sector 2, the garden.** A lawn, a white fence and a bench float in space and grow toward our hull; the player cuts it away or burns it. *Something has read about Earth and copies it badly.*
6. **Sector 3, the sea of wrecks.** The jump stalls, the decks go dark, and every ship ever sent hangs still. Vance: "How can a ship with a higher number than ours be older than us?" *They all came this way.*
7. **Sector 3, becoming a spore.** The garden's pollen got into Mira, and for one minute the player drifts as a spore through the wrecks. *Mira comes back a little changed, and so does how we see the light.*
8. **Sector 5, the station.** On a dark moon, a relay built by crews who came for the disc; its screen says SENT, but the disc is still in the rack. We take it almost without thinking. *They died believing they had sent it home.*
9. **Sector 4, Eden.** A warm world of Earth grass, a cold stream, everyone healed; Jaxon wants to stay, and the player lets him or brings him back. *The kindest place out here only asks you to stop.*
10. **Sector 5, after the station, the twin.** A ship exactly like ours flies beside us a moment late, then holds still: four of us wave, one seat is empty. *The light has read us too, and got us wrong by one.*
11. **Sector 6, the light.** It fills the windows and gives no heat; A.U.R.A. says "five crew" for the first time. A last log from the station crews: "It must go home. Nothing out here may have it." *They all died for this disc, and now it is in our hold.*
12. **The choice.** Cut the disc apart: Earth stays hidden, but its hello is gone forever, and every copy the light made goes dark, Eden and anyone we left there too.
    Or take it home as Earth wanted: now we want something, so the light finally sees us, and it follows us home.
    *(Optional extra: the hole, a starless patch where distress calls go quiet one by one.)*

## 3. Under the water (the writers keep this; the player never needs it explained)
- The second disc was a proud hello; the warning that changed Earth's mind came from the future (the 2029 message). The player may find it; nobody explains it.
- The ships that carried the real order (after 15 or 20, nobody knows which; ship 9 was never one) had a newer drive that threw them further into the past, so their wrecks have higher numbers and are older than us.
- The light is neither evil nor wise: it wants the disc because every crew that reached it wanted the disc. We do not, so we barely register.
- Old stories as a guide, never named on screen: the Sirens (a song that wrecks ships, and you live by not giving in), Pandora's box (the disc), the Lotus-eaters (Eden).
- Open: why A.U.R.A. says four.

## Questions for the designer (each with our recommended answer)
1. **Why does A.U.R.A. say four?** Recommend: decide it before writing sector 6, but never explain it on screen. The payoff is only what the player sees: the twin's empty seat, then "five crew" at the light.
2. **Use the old stories as a guide?** Recommend yes, for writers only. They keep the moments pointing the same way (temptation you must refuse), without anyone naming them.
3. **Does cutting the disc turn Eden dark?** Recommend yes. It is what makes the Eden choice cost something later. It needs the "Jaxon stays" branch built.
4. **The spore minute is new. Build it?** Recommend yes, as a short drift with no way to fail. If there is no time, make it a short film.
5. **If time is short, what goes first?** Recommend the hole (now an optional extra), then the spore. Keep the head count, first wreck, ring of ships, sea of wrecks, garden, Eden, twin, light and the choice.
