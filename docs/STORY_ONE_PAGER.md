# SILENT EXODUS — story and structure (draft 1)

Edit anything. Short on purpose. Plain words on purpose.

## The lie and the truth

**What the crew is told:** Earth is dying. Find a world, settle it, call home.

**What is true:** long ago humanity sent a golden disc into space — a map back to Earth. Later we learned that something is out there, in the direction the disc is flying. If it finds the disc, it finds us. So every mission, including this one, is really sent to **catch the disc before anything else does**. Nobody on board knows. Only **A.U.R.A.** knows.

**Why nobody can call home:** the jump drive moves you through space *and* time. Years pass on Earth with every jump. The crew was told the radio works. It never will.

**The wait calculation (the heart of the game):** a ship launched later, with better engines, overtakes a ship launched earlier. So Earth keeps launching — hundreds, thousands — each one passing the last. That is why the road is full of wrecks, and why **the wrecks get older the further you go**. The disc is the oldest human thing out there. It is at the end.

## Six sectors, one beat each

Each sector has about 4 places. You only have supplies to visit **2**. What you skip is gone.

| # | Sector | Wrecks here are from… | The beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Graveyard | the last 30 years — ships like yours | Normal work. But why are there so many? |
| 2 | The Dark Void | ~100 years ago — old stations | A station log mentions "the disc". A.U.R.A. changes the subject. |
| 3 | The Signal | ~200 years ago | **The truth comes out.** Instruments lie. A.U.R.A. is caught leaving things out. |
| 4 | The Garden | a crew that stopped and settled | The temptation: they quit the chase and lived. Did it cost Earth anything? |
| 5 | The Event Horizon | the first crews, 300+ years | A crew that knew the truth and kept building, *wanting* to be found. |
| 6 | The Threshold | the disc | The last choice. |

## The crew (same people every run)

| Who | Wants | Afraid of | Secret | Pulls you toward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cmdr. Cora Moon** (you) | to get them through | choosing wrong | — | whatever you choose |
| **Tech Mira Chen**, 29 — the believer | to trust A.U.R.A. completely | being alone with a decision | she gives A.U.R.A. the crew's private logs | letting A.U.R.A. decide |
| **Dr. Aris Novak**, 38 — the good one | nobody left behind | becoming someone who walks past | she left her sister behind to take this seat | answering every call, even when it costs |
| **Eng. Jaxon Mercer**, 42 — the settler | to stop, on the best world he can find | that it will never be over | his wife flew an earlier mission; her ship is somewhere ahead | settling early |
| **Spc. Kael Vance**, 45 — the soldier | to finish the job | A.U.R.A. | he was told half the truth, and carries a code that shuts A.U.R.A. down | guarding the disc, trusting no one |
| **A.U.R.A.** — the ship | to reach the disc first | the crew stopping | everything | finishing the mission, with or without you |

Mira and Vance pull against each other (trust the machine / kill the machine). Aris and Jaxon pull against each other (keep helping / stop and live). Each sector gives one choice between two of them.

## Endings (decided by who you listened to, not by dice)

1. **Settle** (Jaxon) — stop at a world. A small honest life. How good depends on the world. The trail stays open.
2. **Erase** (A.U.R.A. / Mira) — catch the disc and destroy it. Earth is safe and will never know your names.
3. **Guard** (Vance) — catch it and keep it. Shut A.U.R.A. down. Become the lighthouse at the end of the road.
4. **Answer** (Aris) — add a new message to the disc, a true one this time, and let it go on.
5. **Overtaken** — too slow. A newer ship passes you. You become a wreck in someone else's Sector 1.
6. **Asleep** — you let A.U.R.A. do everything. She completes the mission. The crew never wakes up.

## Three things the game is quietly about

- **Start now, or wait for better tools?** (the wait calculation)
- **When is it right to stop?** (sunk cost — Jaxon's question)
- **What do you leave behind, and who is it for?** (the disc, the logs, the pods in your cargo hold)

## Questions only you can answer

1. Is the thing that follows ever seen, or only felt? (I suggest: never seen.)
2. Can Jaxon find his wife's ship? (I suggest: yes, Sector 2 or 3, only if you visit the right wreck.)
3. Is A.U.R.A. a villain, or someone doing a terrible job kindly? (I suggest: the second.)
4. Should one run be able to see everything, or should it take 2–3 runs? (I suggest: 2–3, runs are ~25 minutes.)
