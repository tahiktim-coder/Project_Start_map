# Three twists for the light (2026-10-14)

From a writers room of five pitches (fake Eden, the light as an AI, a siren myth, we are the ghosts, Earth made it), scored by two critics. Pick one; nothing is in the game yet.

**1. Eden Is the Light**
- **Twist:** The light is Eden, the place the launch film promised. It looks like home, and every crew that reached it lay down in the grass and stayed.
- **What you see:** The glare clears onto a meadow with the garden's fence and bench. Hundreds of our people lie smiling in the grass with dust on their visors. Then the camera pulls back until the meadow is one cold dot of light.
- **Four crew:** It has no meaning. If Jaxon stays behind, A.U.R.A. says "All four crew aboard", and for once she is right.
- **Stay:** Rest warm with them, and Earth sends another ship.
- **Go home:** Take the disc home along the same road every wreck died on.
- **Build:** One new crowd of still figures lying in the grass. Everything else is reused art. The wrecks are flipped so they face home.

**2. Home at the Far End**
- **Twist:** The light is a cold copy of our own Sun and Earth, and the eight ships before us landed there and stayed.
- **What you see:** The glare dims to a Sun, a blue planet and a moon, framed exactly like the launch film's last shot. We land on the launch pad. The same families are waving, and ships 1 to 8 stand whole. Cora's breath fogs in full sunlight.
- **Four crew:** It has no meaning. The Jaxon line from card 1 works here too.
- **Stay:** Live in a perfect picture of home.
- **Go home:** 61 more years to the real Earth, which is worse than its picture.
- **Build:** One new sky shot. The pad and the families come straight from the film.

**3. The Oldest Wreck**
- **Twist:** Our ship died while we slept, and we are flying it as ghosts toward the light the dying see.
- **What you see:** The twin ship stops. Its windows are frosted, and inside are five frozen faces in the sleep pods: ours. Mira dates it with the disc map, and it is older than every wreck.
- **Four crew:** Dropped. The line becomes "All crew are awake."
- **Into the light:** Rest at last, and the disc is lost.
- **Turn back:** Carry the disc home and never rest.
- **Build:** Frost over the existing pod art, plus one more dating result.

**Recommend #1, Eden Is the Light.** Both critics scored it highest. It is your own "fake Eden called Eden" idea, and it rests on one rule (it looks like home, and nobody leaves) that the player sees and is never told. It is almost all reused art.

There are two gaps to fix before writing it:
- **The ship count.** The film says "eight went before you", but the sea of wrecks is hundreds of our ships.
- **Two places that both feel like Eden.** The sector 4 warm world and the meadow at the light look alike. To fix this, A.U.R.A. says at sector 4: "This is not Eden. Eden is two sectors on."

**Best single line from the other pitches (fixes the ship count):** the sea of wrecks: every hull reads EXODUS 9. Vance: "They told every one of us we were the ninth."
