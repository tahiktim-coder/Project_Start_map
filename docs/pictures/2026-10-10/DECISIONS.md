# What I need you to decide (pictures: opening-A.png, opening-B.png, jump-A.png, jump-B.png)

1. What happens at the light? I suggest: someone goes out on a tether to meet it, and you choose who, or nobody goes.
2. What did Earth hide from us? I suggest: one thing. It got a strange broken message in 2029 and built the ships around it.
3. Why does A.U.R.A. say "four crew" when there are five? I suggest: every ship before us lost its commander at the light.
4. When does the player work that out? I suggest: one small clue per sector, and nobody ever explains it out loud.
5. Did we send the 2029 message ourselves? I suggest: keep the message, but never say who sent it.
6. The opening: no film (A), or the old film playing on the bridge window after the crew wake (B)? I suggest B.
7. The jumps: keep the flight in its own frame (A), or fly it on the big space view beside our ship (B)? I suggest A for now.
8. The jump into sector 3: it stops halfway, our ship goes dark and A.U.R.A. goes silent for ten seconds? I suggest yes.
9. After each jump, A.U.R.A. finds our clock a little more behind the stars? I suggest yes, but drop it if it feels too obvious.
10. Title line: just "Eight went before you." (drop "None of them called home.")? I suggest yes.
11. The tape found in sector 2: cut it, or make it a page with pieces of the 2029 message? I suggest the page.

Questions 1-5 are the story (details: docs/STORY_QUESTIONS.md). 6-11 are the films (details: docs/OPENING_AND_JUMPS.md).
