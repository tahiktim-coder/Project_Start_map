# The game checker's findings (2026-10-09)

Your words: "run a checker who checks games and sees things a good game shouldn't have, like bad UI".
This page keeps every finding, so nothing is lost. docs/GAME_FLOW.md answers them; the last column says where.

- **How it was checked:** the real game (`index.html?art=dither&mute=1`) played from the title through sector 1 into
  sector 2, and every mockup (game-screen-v3's moments, layouts A to D, crew-hires/ship-life), at 1920x1080, the key
  screens also at 1280x720. Then a second reader checked the first draft of GAME_FLOW.md against the other design docs.
- **Screenshots:** `docs/audit/2026-10-09/` (g = real game, v3 = game-screen-v3, lay = layouts, sl = ship-life).
- **Severity:** critical = ships a hazard or breaks the game's promise; major = breaks a standing rule; minor = polish.
- **What is on this page:** all five lenses: UI and readability (lens 1), the flow review (lens 2), game design
  (lens 3), look and continuity (lens 4) and the first ten minutes as a stranger (lens 5). Lenses 3 to 5 were added
  from the checkers' full results after the first pass; GAME_FLOW.md was written from lenses 1 and 2 plus their summaries.

TARGET SELECTED" box and a log; 456 words on the sector map and 248 in orbit, against 40; one stop chains six popups.

smaller. On "space lost its epicness": v3's sky already has faint galaxies, a planet limb and haze (v3_s2, v3_s1_t9).
It reads as "just a dark background" in the real game, where the map is a flat boxed rectangle.
**On "one by one":** yes. Most real-game problems come from stacking popups at each stop.
