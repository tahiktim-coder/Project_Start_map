# SILENT EXODUS — WORKING STORY SPINE

*Draft written with ChatGPT from STORY_BRIEF.md, pasted in by the designer on 2026-10-03. Kept verbatim. LOCKED = part of the
story; LIKELY = preferred direction; OPEN = not to be explained yet.*

## Purpose of this document
This is the current working explanation for the central mystery of Silent Exodus. It is not final canon in every detail. The purpose is to give the game a coherent underlying reality so that scenes, found pages, wrecks, minigames and dialogue can be written consistently while some mysteries are still being developed. Anything marked LOCKED should be treated as part of the story. Anything marked LIKELY is the preferred current direction but can still change. Anything marked OPEN should not be given a definitive explanation yet.

## 1. THE CORE IDEA — LOCKED
Every Exodus ship travels forward through space and backward through time. All Exodus ships experience this. However, the amount of backward displacement changes dramatically between generations of the drive. EXODUS 1–9 use roughly the same first-generation drive. Their temporal displacement is relatively similar, so their launch order remains approximately intact. EXODUS-1 arrives first. Then 2. Then 3. And so on. EXODUS-9 arrives after 1–8. This is why EXODUS-9 can encounter EXODUS 1–8 as relatively recent wrecks. After EXODUS-9 leaves Earth, a new generation of warp drive is introduced. The new drive is believed to be faster, safer and more advanced. It is also much more experimental. The unintended consequence is that later Exodus ships are thrown much farther backward in time. Therefore ships launched long after EXODUS-9 can already have been dead for hundreds of years when EXODUS-9 encounters them. This is the foundation of the temporal graveyard.

## 2. EARLY FORESHADOWING OF THE NEW DRIVE — LIKELY
Very early in the game, before the mystery begins, someone should casually mention EXODUS-10. For example, Jaxon: "Ten gets the new drive. Faster and safer. We missed it by one ship." Do not emphasize this. It should sound like ordinary crew conversation. Later, when the player discovers hull 212 or hull 980 already dead, they remember: those ships were built after us. The innocent opening line becomes evidence. Do not explain the connection immediately.

## 3. EARTH'S TIMELINE
From Earth's perspective everything initially looks normal.

```
1977  Voyager launches with the Golden Record.
2029  Earth receives a strange damaged communication.
Much later  The Exodus programme begins.
EXODUS-1 … EXODUS-9
EXODUS-9 leaves Earth.
New experimental drive generation begins.
EXODUS-10, 11, 12 … 200 … 980 … 6,000 … 20,000 … 40,000+
```

From Earth, the hull numbers are perfectly chronological. The paradox only appears after the ships jump.

## 4. WHAT HAPPENS AFTER THE JUMP
EXODUS 1–9: their temporal displacement is similar enough that their order mostly survives (Earth launch order 1→9; local arrival order 1→9). They are all displaced backward in time, but not radically differently from one another. Therefore 1–8 genuinely existed along the route before 9 arrived. Their wrecks are relatively fresh.

EXODUS 10 AND LATER: the new drives make much stronger jumps. They travel farther through space. They are also thrown vastly farther backward through time. Earth launches EXODUS-900 after EXODUS-9, but EXODUS-900 may arrive 100 years before EXODUS-9. EXODUS-20,000 launched far later may arrive 300 years before EXODUS-9. EXODUS-40,000 launched much later still may arrive 400 years before EXODUS-9. Therefore when EXODUS-9 encounters them, they are ancient.

## 5. THE LOCAL TIMELINE
Approximately what exists along the route from EXODUS-9's perspective (numbers adjustable): ~400 years before 9, hulls around 30,000–40,000 arrive; ~300 years before, hulls 9,000–22,000; ~200 years before, thousands-level hulls; ~100 years before, hundreds-level hulls; ~10–30 years before, EXODUS 1–8; NOW, EXODUS-9 arrives; AFTER EXODUS-9, no later Exodus ship ever arrives. Earth continues launching ships after EXODUS-9, but all of those ships arrive before EXODUS-9 in local time. Therefore EXODUS-9 IS THE FINAL EXODUS ARRIVAL. Not the final launch. The final arrival.

## 6. THE LIGHT'S PERSPECTIVE — LOCKED CONCEPT
The Light experiences ordinary local time. From its perspective EXODUS 1–8 are not humanity's first visitors; they are among humanity's last. 400 years ago EXODUS-40,000 arrives; 350 years ago EXODUS-20,000; 200 years ago EXODUS-6,000; 100 years ago EXODUS-900; 20 years ago EXODUS 1–8; today EXODUS-9; then nobody comes. The Light experiences human civilization almost backwards: the first human ships it encounters have enormous hull numbers, then the numbers broadly decrease, the ships become progressively older in design and more primitive, eventually the first generation, then 1–8, finally 9. Then humanity stops arriving. This should probably never be explained in one speech. The player should eventually be able to realize it.

## 7. WHY EXODUS-9 IS SPECIAL
EXODUS-9 is not chosen. Its crew is not uniquely intelligent. It does not have the best ship. Its advantage is information: it arrives into THE MOST INFORMATION-RICH VERSION OF THE ROUTE THAT WILL EVER EXIST, for two reasons.

FIRST — EXODUS 1–8 BUILD AN ACCIDENTAL TRAIL (LOCKED DIRECTION). Each of the first eight missions gets a little farther than the previous one, not by coordinating, simply because each encounters evidence of previous failures. EXODUS-1 dies; 2 encounters something left by 1, avoids one mistake, gets farther, dies; 3 has information from 1 and 2; and so on. By the time EXODUS-9 follows the route, eight previous failures have unintentionally mapped much of the early journey. Their wrecks, logs, landing sites and decisions form a breadcrumb trail. EXODUS-9 succeeds because eight crews already paid for the early mistakes.

## 8. THEN SOMETHING CHANGES
Once EXODUS-9 passes the frontier reached by EXODUS-8, it finds ships with hull numbers higher than 9: 212, then 980, then thousands, then tens of thousands. And these wrecks are much older. The source of information changes: early, the past helps EXODUS-9; later, the future helps EXODUS-9. EXODUS-9 exists at the point where information from both sides of humanity's history is available simultaneously.

## 9. WHY DID THE BETTER FUTURE SHIPS NOT SOLVE EVERYTHING?
Not "they were all weaker". Many later ships were substantially better; some travelled farther, survived longer, reached the Light. Reaching the Light does not mean understanding. Their disadvantage: when EXODUS-20,000 arrives hundreds of years before EXODUS-9, the wrecks of 1–8 do not exist yet. Later Earth gives them better machines; time gives them less history. EXODUS-9 has primitive hardware but arrives after practically every relevant piece of evidence has been created.

## 10. FUTURE SHIPS SHOULD HAVE DIFFERENT FATES
Do not imply forty thousand ships were all destroyed by the same force. Ships failed during jumps, crashed, ran out of energy, lost crew, settled, abandoned the mission, were stranded, failed landing, turned around, reached deeper sectors, reached the Light, encountered whatever being "read" means. The graveyard should feel like the remains of an impossible human programme, not the victims of one predator.

## 11. THE GOLDEN RECORD — LOCKED FOR CURRENT VERSION
There is only one actual Voyager Golden Record, launched 1977. No copies, none aboard every Exodus, no plot retrieving Voyager or passing the disc between ships. The physical disc does not currently need to be at the Light. The important element is the coordinate system engraved on its cover.

## 12. WHY THE VOYAGER MAP MATTERS
The pulsar map: humanity created a way of describing where something is and approximately when the description was made, using astronomical references. This idea becomes central to the Exodus programme.

## 13. THE 2029 MESSAGE — OPEN
Earth receives an impossible damaged communication, incomplete, possibly fragmented or reordered. Crucial idea: THE MESSAGE USES THE SAME PULSAR REFERENCE LANGUAGE AS THE VOYAGER RECORD. Scientists recognize it. But the pulsar measurements correspond to impossible values describing another location and/or time. This convinces Earth that the message is real.

## 14. HOW EARTH MISUNDERSTANDS THE MESSAGE — OPEN
Avoid the cheap "DO NOT SEND → SEND" version. Earth correctly understands the astronomical data and incorrectly understands the intention. E.g. the original meant "Use the record map. This is where we are. Do not follow us." but fragments imply "RECORD MAP / THIS IS WHERE / FOLLOW US" (placeholder only). The tragedy: Earth correctly discovers where the message came from and incorrectly concludes it is being told to go there.

## 15. THE BOOTSTRAP SHAPE — LIKELY
EXODUS-9 may be revealed as the source of the 2029 warning: 2029 message → Earth decodes coordinates → Exodus programme → 1–9 launch → new drive → later ships thrown back → all their history exists when 9 travels → 9 discovers the graveyard → understands → sends the warning → it reaches 2029 damaged → the loop closes. Nobody changes history. One timeline. Classic bootstrap paradox.

## 16. THE DISC / PULSAR DATING MINIGAME
Stays. The ship holds an archival image/data of the Voyager cover (not another disc). Every Exodus navigation system records pulsar measurements. Boarding a wreck recovers its final astronomical reading. The minigame compares the wreck's final pulsar data against the Voyager 1977 reference: TIME (how much periods changed) and POSITION (how geometry changes from another location). When all fourteen align, the player establishes where the ship was and when it recorded that sky.

## 17. WHY THE MINIGAME EXISTS IN-UNIVERSE
1977 Voyager pulsar map → 2029 message uses the same language → Earth studies it → pulsar-based comparison becomes foundational Exodus science → every Exodus records pulsar data → EXODUS-9 uses those records to date the impossible wrecks. One concept connects Voyager, the warning, the programme, the minigame and the reveal.

## 18. HOW THE PLAYER SHOULD LEARN THE TIME TWIST
In steps: 1) eight ships went before us and died; 2) they followed the same route, each a little farther; 3) more wrecks than eight; 4) a hull number higher than 9 — impossible; 5) dating proves it is also extremely old; 6) more examples: higher numbers = launched later, arrived earlier; 7) later drive generations were thrown farther back; 8) EXODUS-9 is the last arrival; 9) the Light has been seeing humanity arrive backwards. Simple version: later ships were thrown farther back.

## 19. HOW THIS FITS THE SIX SECTORS
S1 The Graveyard: the first eight died here; fresh first-generation wrecks; same route; begin the breadcrumb idea; not the 40,000 scale. S2 The Dark Void: how much did Earth really know?; destabilize the official story; corridor may hear more beacons; hints of other hulls, not explained. S3 The Signal: "this wreck should not exist yet"; dating reveals EXODUS-980 ~100 years dead; a major "what the f***" moment. S4 The Garden: how far does this go?; thousands; older wrecks; systematic; new-drive information becomes relevant. S5 The Tally: Earth never stopped sending ships; the launch ledger; compare launch order vs local death dates; inversion undeniable; EXODUS-9 may be revealed as the final local arrival. S6 The Light: what were all these ships approaching?; very old advanced ships reached here before; better ships reached the end but did not solve it; after EXODUS-9 nothing else is coming.

## 20. THE CORRIDOR / WARP MINIGAME
Early: few wrecks. Later: hundreds, thousands, tens of thousands of beacons, but only one or two ships seen closely. The horror is statistical: empty black space, one hull drifting, a counter saying 18,427 signals detected.

## 21. FIRST-GENERATION WRECKS VS FUTURE WRECKS
1–8: recent, recognizable, similar interiors, personal, peers ("this could have been us"). Future ships: increasingly old, higher numbers, more advanced architecture, degraded by centuries. "The more advanced the ship, the older its corpse."

## 22. WHY EARTH KEEPS SENDING SHIPS — OPEN / IMPORTANT
Needs a satisfying explanation. Do not casually claim Earth sent forty thousand missions despite receiving nothing. Ingredients: each mission transmits a little before silence; A.U.R.A.'s twin may give limited data; each mission appears to improve; Earth believes it is getting closer; the experimental drive keeps promising; data from later missions may arrive in strange temporal order. Highest-priority unresolved question.

## 23. WHY THE LIGHT DOES NOT SIMPLY COME TO EARTH — OPEN
Do not assume reading human ships gives Earth's exact location. Knowing what Earth looks like differs from an astronomical coordinate solution. The pulsar map encodes location; this may matter later.

## 24. WHAT THE LIGHT IS — PARTIALLY LOCKED
Looks like a distant warm star; is not a normal sun; no warmth; reads things that reach it; may reproduce what it has completely read; patient, not predatory. Not an alien empire, a monster, a villain giving speeches, or a civilization broadcasting. The consequence of being read remains adjustable and must become concrete enough for the ending to matter.

## 25. THE FOUR CREW / FIVE PEOPLE MYSTERY — OPEN
Stays a central hook. Must fit one timeline, matter emotionally, pay off near the finale, not make A.U.R.A. evil or malfunctioning, keep every other number correct. Leave unresolved for now.

## 26. IMPORTANT THEMATIC CONSEQUENCE
Not "humanity sent bad ships until one good crew succeeded". Future humanity builds better ships, thrown into emptier versions of the route's history. The advanced ships have technology. EXODUS-9 has history.

## 27. THE MOST IMPORTANT PARADOXICAL IMAGE
From Earth: 1 → 9 → 10 → 100 → 1,000 → 10,000 → 40,000. From the Light: 40,000-ish → 20,000-ish → thousands → hundreds → 1–8 → 9 → nothing. Broad trend, not exact reverse order.

## 28. THINGS THE STORY SHOULD NOT DO
No alternate timelines; nobody changes history; EXODUS-9 not chosen; future ships not worse; not all forty thousand die the same way; no monster hunting ships; no multiple Golden Records; no retrieving Voyager; no passing one disc ship to ship; Earth's mistake not only losing "DO NOT"; no exposition speech about the Light's perspective; characters do not immediately understand the high-hull wrecks; the player should sometimes be ahead of the crew.

## 29. VARIABLES THAT ARE SAFE TO CHANGE LATER
A launch era after 2029; B gap between missions; C exact displacement (20/100/200/300/400 are approximations); D why the new drive causes the extreme throw; E what information reaches Earth from each mission; F why Earth sends tens of thousands; G exact 2029 warning; H why the warning is corrupted (lost packets, reordering, incomplete state transmission, pieces surviving) — needs one simple rule; I A.U.R.A.'s twin; J what being read costs; K why EXODUS-9 sends the warning; L four versus five.

## 30. CURRENT STORY SPINE IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Humanity receives a damaged impossible message in 2029 using the same pulsar language once placed on the Voyager Golden Record. Earth eventually interprets that message as a reason to send the Exodus programme along a specific route. Every Exodus jump moves forward in space and backward in time. EXODUS 1–9 use roughly the same drive generation, so their chronology remains mostly intact; each of the first eight gets slightly farther than the last, leaving an accidental trail that helps EXODUS-9. After 9 launches, a supposedly faster and safer experimental drive causes much stronger backward displacement. Tens of thousands of later ships are therefore thrown centuries into the route's past. When EXODUS-9 travels deeper, it finds increasingly advanced ships with impossible future hull numbers that have already been dead for hundreds of years. Some failed, some settled, some reached the Light, but all contributed evidence. EXODUS-9 ultimately arrives into the most information-rich version of the route that will ever exist: the failures of its immediate predecessors lie behind it, and the wrecks of humanity's future lie ahead. From the Light's perspective, humanity has been arriving for centuries in broadly reverse technological order, ending with EXODUS-9. After 9, no other human ship will ever arrive.

## 31. THE FEELING WE ARE AIMING FOR
Not "wow, complicated time travel", but: "Oh. Those ships were from our future. They got here before us. The thing has been seeing us arrive backwards. We are the last ones." The machinery can be complex; the realization should be simple.
